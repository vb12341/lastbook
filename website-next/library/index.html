In every library size

Examples from Small, also included in Medium and All. PDFs include books, manuals and selected chapters.

Injury and illness

First aid, caring for the sick, medicines, teeth, birth and mental health.

  • Selected chapters from Where There Is No Doctor and Where There Is No Dentist (Hesperian)
  • Basic Emergency Care (World Health Organization)
  • MSF Clinical Guidelines and Essential Drugs (Médecins Sans Frontières)
  • Videos: hands-only CPR, using an AED, choking, tourniquets

Water and toilets

Make water safe, clean wells and tanks, and deal with waste.

  • WHO technical notes on drinking water, wells and toilets in emergencies
  • Public Health Engineering in Precarious Situations (MSF)
  • Two Ways to Purify Water (National Park Service)

Food

Store, can, dry and freeze food safely, and grow more of it.

  • Canning, drying, freezing and pickling guides (National Center for Home Food Preservation)
  • Seeds in Emergencies (FAO)
  • Videos: water-bath and pressure canning, seed saving

Power, tools and repair

Generators, batteries and solar, small engines, tools and building.

  • Generator and engine owner’s manuals (Honda, Generac)
  • Solar wiring and battery guides (Victron Energy, US Department of Energy)
  • Tools and Their Uses and Wood Handbook
  • Videos: welding, small-engine repair, woodworking

Getting ready for a disaster

Plans and checklists for floods, fire, storms, heat, earthquakes and power cuts.

  • Are You Ready? and the Emergency Supply Kit Checklist (FEMA)
  • Be Prepared and Disaster Preparedness for Older Adults (American Red Cross)
  • Weather safety sheets (NOAA National Weather Service)

Radio and finding your way

Emergency radio, maps and compass.

  • ARES Field Resources Manual and Quickstart Guide (ARRL)
  • Finding Your Way with Map and Compass (USGS)
  • Video: How to Use a Compass (REI)

More in Medium and All

Thousands more documents, including course notes, readings, exercises and technical references.

Science and mathematics

Physics, chemistry, genetics, geology, calculus and statistics.

  • MIT course materials in quantum physics, thermodynamics and genetics
  • Linear algebra, differential equations, probability and statistics

Computing and electronics

How computers and electronic systems work.

  • MIT Introduction to Algorithms and Computation Structures
  • Circuits, microelectronics and computer-system engineering

Engineering and infrastructure

Materials, heat, fluids and water systems.

  • Advanced fluid mechanics and heat-transfer course materials
  • Water and wastewater treatment manuals

Making and manufacturing

Tools, materials and production methods.

  • Metalworking and materials references
  • Ceramics, clothing and textile materials

Transport and equipment

Technical references for operating and maintaining equipment.

  • Aviation handbooks, including instructor materials
  • Generator, engine and machinery manuals

More video teaching in All

All adds a much larger video collection alongside the documents.

  • Science, engineering and practical-skills videos
  • Text-only Wikipedia is included in All

Encyclopedias

MDWiki has plain-language articles on conditions, medicines and first aid. Appropedia covers water, sanitation, energy and building projects.

Medium also includes Wikibooks. All adds Wikipedia, without pictures.

These are written by volunteers. They’re good for background. For steps where safety matters, use the guides and manuals.

The map

A map of the whole US with main roads, towns and water. Find a town, ZIP code or place with no signal.

Small includes this US base map. Mac setup also downloads your selected state’s detailed streets, buildings and local places. Available address indexes add offline street-address search. Coverage varies by state. The maps show recorded geography, not live damage, closures or hazards.
