Privacy and trust

Lastbook works offline after setup. Your library and AI conversations stay on your computer.

The app works offline after setup

Setup downloads your chosen library and optional AI model. After setup, the library and AI run on your computer. There is no account to make.

What you save in Lastbook stays on your computer.

You can check: turn off Wi‑Fi and open Lastbook. Everything works the same.

This site doesn’t track you

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No fonts, scripts or pictures from other companies. Everything on these pages comes from lastbook.org.

The buying guide contains Amazon affiliate links. If you click one, Amazon receives the request and its own privacy and cookie policies apply. Lastbook may earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The site and downloads are hosted by Cloudflare, which may keep standard request logs, such as IP addresses.

Email updates

When you subscribe, we store your email address and signup date in Cloudflare D1. We use them for Lastbook updates. To ask us to remove your address, email hello@lastbook.org.

App signing

The Mac app is signed with our Developer ID and notarized by Apple. The Windows app and installer are signed. Your computer may ask you to confirm opening a downloaded app.
